Call-Outs, General Maintenance, and Repairs

Our standard call-out fee for domestic work is £100, which covers the electrician’s attendance and initial assessment for the first hour. After this, work is charged at £50 per hour for call-outs, general maintenance, and repairs.

Please note that prices may vary for out-of-hours work or Sunday visits. This structure ensures transparency, while allowing us to provide a fair and accurate price based on the specific work required.

EICR - Electrical Installation Condition Report

Our pricing for Domestic EICRs typically ranges between £200 – £250. The exact price depends on property size and the number of circuits or consumer units. Larger properties with more wiring and electrical outlets take longer to test, so the price may be higher. Another factor that can affect pricing is the age and condition of the installation. Older, damaged, or deteriorating electrical systems may require a larger amount of sampling to produce a detailed and accurate report.

All our consumer units are installed to the highest standards, ensuring both safety and reliability. Each unit is fully loaded with RCBOs to provide individual circuit protection, along with a Surge Protection Device (SPD) to safeguard your electrical system from voltage spikes. PAT Testing

Our minimum call-out fee is £100, which applies if PAT testing is the only work at the property. This covers travel, setup, and certification time. Additional appliances are charged at £3 per item. If we are already working on site, the first ten appliances are charged at £70, with £3 per item thereafter. This includes a visual inspection, electrical test, and a certificate issued upon completion.

Emergency Light Testing

Our emergency lighting tests are designed to ensure compliance with BS 5266-1 and BS EN 50172. Each test includes visual inspections, functional checks, battery discharge tests, and certification. Prices are indicative, as every site is unique. A site visit is always recommended for an accurate quotation.

Small sites (up to ~10 lights)£200 – £250 Standard 3-hour test, includes certification. Covers offices, small shops, or residential blocks.

Medium sites (10 – 40 lights) £250 – £400 Includes multiple floors or larger layouts. Price may vary depending on access and number of circuits.

Large sites (40+ lights) £400 – £700+ Multi-floor offices, hotels, industrial units, or large retail spaces. May require multiple visits.
