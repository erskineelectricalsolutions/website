Welcome

27 June 2024 · Keir Erskine

Welcome to "A Day in the Life"... Of an Electrician.

Hello and welcome, I'm excited to take you on a journey through my world of electrical work, sharing the ins and outs of what it means to be an electrician. Whether you are simply curious about what we do or already a fellow tradesperson, then this blog is for you.

Who Am I?

Let me introduce myself. My name is Keir, and I've been working as an electrician for over 11 years. Over time, I've tackled a wide variety of electrical projects, from residential installations and commercial wiring to fault finding complex systems. Working in a number of environments from airports, hospitals, high schools to domestic dwellings allowing me to accumulate a wide range of knowledge over the years.

What to Expect

In this blog, I'll be sharing my day-in day-out experiences, challenges, and triumphs on the job. Here's a glimpse of what you can expect:

Regular Logs: Detailed accounts of my day-to-day tasks, including the different types of jobs I handle, the tools and techniques I use, and how I solve unexpected problems.

Project Highlights: In-depth looks at some of the more interesting projects I work on and even some of the minor works. You'll get a behind-the-scenes look at how these projects come together from start to finish.

Industry Insights: Updates on the latest trends and technologies in the electrical industry and how these advancements are shaping our work and what the future might hold.

Tips and Tricks: Practical advice and insights for aspiring electricians and DIY enthusiasts. From safety protocols to efficiency hacks covering a range of topics. Who knows? You might actually learn something new!

Q&A Sessions: Opportunities for you to ask questions and get answers. Whether you're curious about a specific aspect of electrical work or need advice on a project you're tackling.

Why This Blog?

Being an electrician is a profession that requires a unique blend of technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, and hands-on expertise. By sharing my experiences, I hope to inspire others to appreciate the art and science behind electrical work.

Thank you for visiting, I look forward to sharing my journey with you and hope you find this blog both informative and enjoyable.

Stay safe

Keir Erskine
