About Erskine Electrical Solutions

Electrical work for homes and businesses across Fife, Edinburgh, the Lothians, Dundee, Stirling and Falkirk.

Contact us to discuss your project, request a quote or ask about qualifications and insurance.

Our Expertise

We pride ourselves on delivering top-quality electrical services for projects of all sizes, from minor residential repairs to large-scale commercial installations. Dedicated to providing reliable, efficient, and safe solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs. Whether it’s a simple wiring fix, a full home rewire, or a complex industrial project, we approach every job with the same level of professionalism and attention to detail.

Qualifications

Fully certified in BS7671 Requirements for Electrical Installations, ensuring full compliance with all current wiring regulations. Additionally, we hold qualifications in BS7671 Periodic Inspection and Testing, enabling us to carry out thorough and accurate assessments of electrical systems. Our expertise extends to approved Health & Safety practices, ensuring all projects are completed in the safest manner possible. We are also certified in First Aid, PASMA (Prefabricated Access Suppliers’ and Manufacturers’ Association), and IPAF (International Powered Access Federation), demonstrating our commitment to safety and operational excellence across all types of electrical work.

Safety and Compliance

At Erskine Electrical Solutions, we pride ourselves on delivering a professional, reliable service where safety and compliance are always the top priority. As a SELECT-registered electrical contractor, all work is carried out to the latest British Standards and Scottish regulations, ensuring every installation is safe, compliant, and built to last. We are fully insured for your peace of mind, and every job is completed with thorough testing, certification, and attention to detail. Our commitment to high standards means you can trust that all electrical work is completed correctly, safely, and with minimal disruption.

SELECT

As a proud member of SELECT, the trade association for the electrical contracting industry in Scotland, we are committed to upholding the highest standards of professionalism, safety, and quality in all our work. SELECT membership provides us with access to ongoing training, technical support, and industry updates, ensuring we stay at the forefront of the latest developments and best practices in the electrical field. Membership also signifies our adherence to stringent codes of practice and a strong commitment to continuous improvement, giving our clients complete confidence in the reliability and excellence of our services.
